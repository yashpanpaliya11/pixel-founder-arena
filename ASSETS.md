# Character art and brand associations

The two bundled roster PNGs were generated with OpenAI ImageGen for this project in November 2026. The project uses CSS viewports to display individual cells from the original sheets. Idle, talking, attacking, and celebration motions are CSS transforms of static art; these are not hand-drawn animation frame sequences.

Characters are fictional, stylized parody depictions. Dialogue is written for the game and must not be represented as real quotations, endorsements, or an accurate simulation of a person's opinions.

Company names and logos identify the associated brand. They remain the marks of their respective owners; the software's MIT license does not grant trademark rights or imply an affiliation. Brand associations are for the characters, not a directory of current executive positions. Jack Dorsey's Twitter association is historical. Generated logo art is illustrative and may differ from official brand artwork.

Included sheets:

- `public/assets/founders.png`: 15 characters, five columns and three rows.
- `public/assets/tech.png`: 10 characters, five columns and two rows.

All assets ship inside the package; nothing is fetched from an external image host at runtime. Custom character packs in version 1 use an initial-letter avatar. Arbitrary remote image URLs and executable pack code are not supported.
